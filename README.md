Sales Data Summary:
Purpose: Tracks product sales with quantity and pricing details.

Fields:

id: Unique identifier for each sale

date: Date of the sale

product: Product name

quantity: Number of units sold

price: Price per unit

 Insights:
Total Quantity Sold: Sum of all units sold across all products

Total Revenue: Sum of quantity × price for all sales

Revenue by Product: Shows which products contribute most to sales

This dataset is ideal for basic sales analysis, product performance tracking, and generating visual summaries like bar charts.
